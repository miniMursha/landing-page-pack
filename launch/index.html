Questions people ask first

  • Will test orders hit my sales?

    No. Nightjar uses a test card your shop marks as a test, and cancels the order straight after.

  • Does it slow my site down?

    Checks run one page at a time between 01:00 and 05:00 in your time zone.

  • Can it log in?

    Yes. Give it a test account and it checks the pages behind the login too.

  • What if nothing breaks?

    Then you get no email. A weekly summary is there if you want one.
